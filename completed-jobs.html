Stamped patio & steps

Covered patio with steps to the driveway

Type
Stamped patio
Finish
Flagstone stamp, charcoal
Features
4 wide steps, post bases
Setting
Timber-frame pavilion

A full stamped patio under a new timber-frame pavilion, with four wide, shallow steps carrying the flagstone pattern down to the driveway. The slab was pitched to drain out from under the roof, the post bases were poured into the slab, and a river-rock border finished the edge where the patio meets the grade.

The charcoal integral color and flagstone texture give it the look of cut stone with none of the joints to shift or weed. Want something like it? See stamped and decorative or patios.
