About Caliber Concrete LLC

Caliber Concrete LLC is a residential concrete contractor based in Gentry, Arkansas, owned and operated by Logan Wiebe.

Logan Wiebe · Owner

Why I started Caliber

I started Caliber Concrete because I kept seeing the same thing: good concrete work in Northwest Arkansas is hard to find, and when you do find it, the guy is booked out and doesn't call back. Most of the complaints I heard from homeowners weren't about cracked slabs. They were about contractors who didn't show up, didn't explain anything, and left a mess.

So the company is built around the stuff that's actually in my control. I answer the phone. I come look at the job before I quote it. I'm on site for the prep, the pour and the finish — not a crew you've never met. And when I leave, the yard looks like a yard again.

I'm not going to tell you I've been doing this for thirty years. I'm going to tell you exactly what the job takes, do it the right way, and stand behind it.

Logan WiebeOwner, Caliber Concrete LLC · Gentry, Arkansas
Our standards

How Caliber runs a job

Owner on every job

The person who gives you the estimate is the person forming, pouring and finishing. There's no hand-off.

Upfront about everything

What's included, what isn't, and the start date. You'll know before the first shovel goes in.

Prep that earns a warranty

Compacted base, steel and correct joints are what keep concrete from cracking. Go with the recommended prep and the work comes with a warranty option.

Clean when we leave

Forms pulled, washout handled, yard put back. The only thing left behind is the concrete.
