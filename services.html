Services

Residential concrete services in Northwest Arkansas

Four kinds of flatwork, each done start to finish by the owner. No repairs, no commercial — just residential concrete poured right.

Driveways & Approaches

New driveways, extensions, turnarounds and approaches. Compacted base, reinforcement, correctly placed joints and a broom finish built for parking on.

Learn more

Patios

Backyard patios, porch slabs and outdoor living areas — broom-finished or stamped and colored. Pitched to drain away from the house.

Learn more

Sidewalks & Walkways

Front walks, side paths, steps and small slabs. Safe to walk on wet, jointed so cracks happen where they're supposed to.

Learn more

Stamped & Decorative

Stamped patterns and integral color for patios, walks and driveway borders. The look of stone or pavers in one solid slab.

Learn more
What we don't do

Residential flatwork only

No commercial work

Caliber is a residential contractor. Homeowners first, plus builders and contractors on residential jobs.

No repair work

We pour new concrete. If your job is a patch or a repair, we'll tell you on the phone and point you the right direction.

No exposed aggregate

Broom, stamped and colored finishes only. We'd rather do three finishes well than six finishes half-way.
