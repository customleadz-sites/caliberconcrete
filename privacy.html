Privacy Policy

Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-09. This page explains what this website collects and what happens to it. It's short because we don't collect much.

What we collect

If you send the estimate form, we receive what you type: your name, phone number, email (if you give it), the town or address of the job, the type and rough size of the job, your timeline, and anything you write in the message box. We use it for one thing — to call you back about your project. We don't sell it, rent it or add you to a mailing list.

If you call or text, your phone number shows up on Logan's phone like any other call.

Who else touches your information

  • Web3Forms delivers the estimate form to our email. The form fields pass through their servers to do that.
  • Google Maps provides the embedded maps, and Google Fonts provides the typefaces. Loading them sends your browser's standard request information to Google.
  • Vercel hosts the site and keeps standard server logs (IP address, pages requested, browser type).
  • OpenStreetMap and CARTO provide the map tiles on the service-area page.

We don't currently run analytics or advertising tags. If that changes, this page will be updated.

Cookies

This site doesn't set cookies of its own. Embedded services above may set their own under their own policies.
