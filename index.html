Questions

Things people ask before they call

Do you do commercial concrete work?

Not right now. Caliber Concrete is residential only — driveways, approaches, patios, sidewalks and stamped or decorative finishes for homeowners, and flatwork for builders and contractors on residential jobs.

Do you do concrete repair?

No. We pour new residential concrete. If you need a repair or a patch we'll tell you straight on the first call so nobody wastes a trip.

How do estimates work?

Call or send the form. Logan will ask a few questions on the phone first — where the job is, roughly what size, what you're picturing — then come out, measure and give you a written estimate. Estimates are free.

How soon can you start?

Usually within about a week of the estimate, depending on weather and what's already on the schedule. You'll get a real date, not a guess.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — Caliber Concrete LLC is licensed and insured.

Where are you based and how far do you go?

Gentry, Arkansas. We work across Northwest Arkansas: Gentry, Siloam Springs, Bentonville, Centerton, Rogers, Springdale and Fayetteville. If you're close to one of those, call and ask.

Ready to pour?

Call, text, or send the quick form. You'll hear back from Logan, not a call center.
